FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account to use CV Lab?

No. You can browse templates, build a resume, preview it, and download PDF and DOCX files entirely without signing up, verifying an email, or paying.

Where is my resume stored?

While you're editing, your work lives in your browser's local storage. If you click Save, it's stored on our server under a session token that only your browser has. The token never leaves your device unless you share your browser or delete it manually.

What if I clear my browser data or switch devices?

Saved resumes are tied to the session token stored in your browser. Clearing browser data or switching devices means your saved resumes won't be recoverable — download your resume first if you need to keep it.

Are the templates really ATS-friendly?

The templates marked ATS are designed to be: single-column, real text, predictable headings, conservative fonts. ATS compatibility also depends on the system an employer uses, so we can't guarantee an individual system will parse perfectly — but our ATS templates use the patterns that parsers handle most reliably.

Are the downloads really free?

Yes. Both PDF and DOCX downloads are free and do not require an account. Premium template styling is available to all users on this site — no paid tier is required to access any part of the builder today.

Can I export to Word for editing?

Yes. The DOCX download is a real, editable Microsoft Word document. Complex multi-column layouts may be simplified in DOCX; single-column templates translate most faithfully.
