Help & Guides

Answers, guidance, and honest limits.

Everything you need to build and download a resume — plus what to do if something goes wrong.

Getting started

  1. Open Templates and pick a design. Every template is free to use on this site.
  2. Click Use template to jump into the builder with that design applied.
  3. Fill in each section. Your preview updates as you type; nothing is uploaded until you press Save.
  4. When you're ready, open the Download menu and choose PDF or DOCX.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Browsing, editing, previewing, saving, and downloading all work without any account, email verification, or payment.

Where is my data stored?

While editing, drafts live in your browser. If you click Save, your resume is stored on our server, keyed to an anonymous session token that only your browser holds. You can delete any saved resume at any time from the builder.

What happens if I switch devices?

Saved resumes are tied to the session token stored in your browser's local storage. Switching browsers or devices means your saved resumes are not accessible there. Download your resume first if you need to move it.

Can I use the same resume for multiple applications?

Yes. Use Duplicate from the builder to copy a resume, then tailor the copy for each application. Duplicates are separate documents; changes to one don't affect the other.

Are the PDF downloads free?

Yes. Both PDF and DOCX downloads are free, with no watermark and no per-export fee.

Writing tips

Lead with impact

Every bullet should answer "so what?" Lead with a result or a number where you have one, then explain how you achieved it.

Use active verbs

Led, shipped, designed, reduced, increased, launched, mentored. Passive verbs ("was responsible for") are weaker and take more space.

Quantify wherever possible

"Reduced latency by 40%", "managed a team of 6", "saved $120k annually". Numbers make claims credible.

Tailor to the role

Mirror the vocabulary used in the job description — especially for skills that appear in the requirements. Recruiters and ATS both scan for those phrases.

ATS guidance

Applicant Tracking Systems parse resumes before a human does. To maximize compatibility:

  • Use an ATS-marked template — single-column, real text, standard headings.
  • Avoid tables, text boxes, icons inside content, and images containing text.
  • Use standard section names: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills.
  • Date format: Jan 2022 – Present reads reliably across parsers.
  • Use a standard font (Inter, Arial, Calibri) — decorative fonts are sometimes ignored.

Troubleshooting

The Download button doesn't work

PDF export uses your browser's print engine. Ensure your browser is up to date, and check that pop-up blocking isn't interfering with the print dialog. If the DOCX download fails, confirm you have a working internet connection — DOCX is generated on the server.

My saved resume disappeared

Saved resumes are tied to the session token in your browser. Clearing browser data or switching devices makes them inaccessible. Local drafts (autosaved) are also stored in your browser and are lost when browser data is cleared.

The preview shows content differently than the PDF

PDF export reproduces the preview exactly, including fonts and spacing, using your browser's print engine. If you see differences, try updating your browser or exporting again from a fresh tab.
